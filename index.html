Why this repo exists

The Shoalwater game and explorer live in SuperInstance/shoalwater — a small project that works beautifully at small scale, and stays that way. This is its heavy sibling: the entire survey extent at full fidelity, 253 MB of verified tiles, with a viewer that adapts to the machine in front of it instead of pretending every device is the same.

How the adaptation works

  • Probe first. Before fetching anything heavy, the page measures the device: GPU rendering available, CPU cores, device memory, screen size, connection speed, and any data-saver setting.
  • Overview instantly. The z0–z1 overview (198 tiles, ~15 MB) always loads — every device gets the whole Sound, truthfully.
  • Refine as capacity allows. Standard devices stream z2 in the background; full-capacity devices continue to z3 — centre-out, so the water you are looking at sharpens first. A progress pill shows the honest state.
  • You can override. The Capacity switch is yours. Asking for more than the device can hold is allowed; it will simply be slow, and the page will say so.
